About KurdNet

KurdNet uses a modified AlexNet-style convolutional neural network trained on a large dataset of Sorani Kurdish handwritten digits and isolated characters. All inference runs locally in your browser via TensorFlow.js, ensuring privacy and low-latency interaction.
